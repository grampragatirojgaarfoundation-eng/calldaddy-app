1. Overview & Who We Are

CallDaddy ("we", "us", "our", "the App") is a QR-code based masked emergency communication platform operated under Indian law. Our registered domain is calldaddy.in.

CallDaddy enables QR code owners to receive masked calls and messages from people who scan their QR code, without revealing the owner's phone number. The App is designed for legitimate emergency use such as vehicle identification, lost-and-found items, and property contact.

📍 Jurisdiction: Republic of India | Governing Law: IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023, CERT-In Guidelines 2022

3. Data We Do NOT Collect or Store

⚠️ The following are NEVER recorded by CallDaddy under any circumstances:

  • 🚫 Voice call recordings — Calls are peer-to-peer (WebRTC). Our servers only relay the connection, never the audio content.
  • 🚫 Text message content — Chat messages exist only in RAM (server memory) and are permanently deleted when the session ends. They are NEVER written to any database or log file.
  • 🚫 Your phone number shown to callers — The person who scans your QR code NEVER receives your phone number. They only receive a temporary session ID.
  • 🚫 Location/GPS data — We do not request or store location data.
  • 🚫 Photos or camera data — We do not access your camera.
  • 🚫 Contacts list — We do not access your device contacts.
  • 🚫 Payment card details — Payment is processed by Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant). We only store the Razorpay Order ID and Payment ID for record-keeping.

5. Data Masking & Privacy Technology

How Masked Calling Works

When someone scans your QR code:

  1. Our server generates a random Session ID (UUID format) — e.g., a3f8b2c1-...
  2. The scanner's browser receives ONLY this Session ID — never your name, phone number, or any personal data
  3. Our server uses the Session ID to look up your QR internally and route the call
  4. WebRTC audio connection is established peer-to-peer — once connected, our server is not involved in the audio
  5. After 60 seconds (or when either party hangs up), the session is destroyed and the Session ID becomes permanently invalid

Phone Number Encryption

All phone numbers stored in our database are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a random 12-byte IV per field. Decryption requires our server's encryption key, which is stored only on the server and never transmitted. Raw phone numbers are never logged anywhere.

7. Security Measures

  • 🔒 HTTPS/TLS 1.3 — All data in transit is encrypted
  • 🔒 AES-256-GCM — Phone numbers encrypted at rest
  • 🔒 JWT httpOnly Cookies — Session tokens cannot be accessed by JavaScript (XSS protection)
  • 🔒 Redis Rate Limiting — Prevents brute force and DDoS attacks
  • 🔒 NoSQL Injection Protection — All inputs sanitized
  • 🔒 SHA-256 IP Hashing — IPs stored as one-way hashes
  • 🔒 Content Security Policy (CSP) — Prevents XSS attacks
  • 🔒 HSTS — Prevents protocol downgrade attacks (1 year)
  • 🔒 Input Validation — All user inputs validated server-side

9. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India), you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data stored by us. Email: privacy@calldaddy.in
  • Right to Correction: Update your profile information at any time from within the app.
  • Right to Erasure: Delete your account from the app settings. Your personal profile data will be deleted. Anonymised legal logs (CERT-In) will be retained as required by law.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Contact our Grievance Officer within 48 hours for any privacy complaint.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent by deleting your account at any time.
  • Right to Nominate: You may nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

Grievance Officer: privacy@calldaddy.in | Response within 48 hours

11. Legal Compliance

CERT-In Guidelines 2022 (India)

As required by CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team) Guidelines dated April 28, 2022, we retain the following data for a minimum of 180 days (we retain 1 year):

  • IP addresses (stored as SHA-256 hashes) of users who initiate calls/scans
  • Timestamps of all connection events
  • QR code identifiers involved in each session
  • Call duration records

This data may be disclosed to law enforcement authorities on receipt of a valid legal order, court order, or government directive.

IT Act 2000 (India)

We comply with Section 43A and Section 72A of the Information Technology Act 2000 regarding protection of sensitive personal data.

Google Play Store & Apple App Store Compliance

This privacy policy complies with Google Play Store Developer Policy, Apple App Store Review Guidelines, and all applicable data protection laws.
